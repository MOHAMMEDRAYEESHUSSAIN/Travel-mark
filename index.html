Our Core Philosophy

Travel Mark was conceived as an independent, traveler-powered platform dedicated to celebrating the authentic soul of India. India is far more than a checklist of generic landmarks—it is a tapestry of over 28 states, 8 union territories, thousands of regional flavours, ancient spiritual lineages, and breathtaking landscapes.

We reject staged commercial tourist traps and sponsored exaggerations. Here, every guide, tip, price estimate, and photograph comes from travelers who have personally walked the stone steps, tasted the street food, and meditated on the river ghats.

Responsible Tourism

We actively advocate for zero-waste travel, respecting indigenous traditions, wearing appropriate attire at sacred temples, mosques, and gurdwaras, and protecting fragile ecosystems across the Himalayas, Western Ghats, and coral archipelagos.

Community Guidelines

To keep Travel Mark an inspiring, welcoming, and truthful home for travelers, all members agree to the following standards:

1. Authentic & Real Experiences Only

Only publish reviews, tips, and photos from trips you have personally undertaken. Do not post fabricated reviews or commercial ads masquerading as travel stories.

2. Deep Respect for Sacred Places & Traditions

India is home to diverse faiths—Hinduism, Islam, Christianity, Sikhism, Buddhism, Jainism, and tribal customs. Always follow local dress codes, remove footwear when required, refrain from unauthorized photography during prayers, and write about all traditions with dignity.

3. Protect Fragile Wildlife & Environments

Never promote illegal wildlife feeding, trespassing on protected tribal zones, or littering plastics. Do not publish exact GPS coordinates of delicate, non-tourist wildlife habitats.

4. Safety and Constructive Community

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, hate speech, and spam result in immediate account restriction. Help our moderation team by reporting violating posts.

Privacy Policy

Travel Mark is committed to safeguarding your personal data. We strictly collect only minimal data needed to operate your explorer account and store your travel plans.

Data Protection Principles

  • We never sell or rent your personal contact information to third parties.
  • Precise live GPS tracking is never collected without your explicit voluntary permission.
  • Uploaded photographs have sensitive EXIF metadata stripped to protect your personal privacy.
  • You may edit, export, or delete your account data at any time from your profile settings.
